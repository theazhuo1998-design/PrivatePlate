# Data license status

No open-data license has been granted yet.

The files under `fixtures/` are AI-assisted synthetic demonstration data created
specifically for the PrivatePlate prototype. Their provenance is documented in
`fixtures/PROVENANCE.md` and `fixtures/MANIFEST.json`; they are not redistributed
clinical datasets, measured nutrition databases, medical advice, or real
household records.

Nutrition and quantity values in the fixtures are engineering estimates for
demonstration only. Public redistribution is not authorized until the owner
selects and approves a final data license and the manifest records that decision.

The production food database and the advanced chronic-disease data assets are
not part of this repository.
