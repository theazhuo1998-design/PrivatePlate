# Data license status

No open-data license has been granted yet.

The files under `fixtures/` are staged as synthetic demonstration data while
their provenance is reviewed. They must not be presented as clinical data,
measured nutrition facts, medical advice, or real household records. Public
redistribution is not authorized until the owner approves a final data license
and the manifest records that decision.

The production food database and the advanced chronic-disease data assets are
not part of this repository.
